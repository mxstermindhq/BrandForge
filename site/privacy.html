Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

BrandForge (“we”) operates brandforge.gg. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it when you browse our site or contact us about packages.

What we collect

  • Contact conversations — messages, usernames, and files you send on Discord or Telegram when inquiring about work.

We do not run a checkout or account system on brandforge.gg. We do not sell personal information.

How we use data

  • To prevent abuse and secure our infrastructure.

Retention

Project files and conversation history are kept as long as needed for delivery, support, and legal obligations. Analytics data is retained per Cloudflare’s policies.

Your choices
